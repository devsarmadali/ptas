# Minimum Screen Inventory

## Shared

- Sign in, MFA and account recovery
- Role-aware home/work queue
- Global taxpayer/demand search
- Notifications and task inbox
- Access-denied and jurisdiction-change states

## Inspector/field

- Assigned-circle dashboard
- Survey/new party capture
- Duplicate-candidate review
- Taxpayer/case workspace
- Annual statement and assessment wizard
- Evidence upload and offline sync status
- Notice issue and service status
- Payment slip entry and exception follow-up

## ETO/assessing authority

- Approval queue
- Assessment comparison/revision review
- Hearing/service evidence review
- Decision and reason screen
- Penalty, withdrawal, discontinuance, appeal and adjustment workspaces
- User/jurisdiction oversight subject to policy

## Director/management

- Demand and recovery overview
- District/circle comparison
- Pending decisions and aging
- Legal/configuration exception dashboard

## Administration/operations

- Organization and assignment masters
- Effective-dated legal configuration
- Form-template versioning
- Integration and reconciliation console
- Audit search and export
- Job, queue, backup and service-health dashboard
