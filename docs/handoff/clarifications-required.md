# Clarifications Required Before Production

The bootstrap can proceed without answers to these questions. Production activation cannot.

1. Which department-controlled GitHub organization and Vercel organization will own the project?
2. What official domain/subdomain will be used, and who owns DNS and certificates?
3. Which cloud region is legally and operationally acceptable for taxpayer data and backups?
4. Which organizational identity provider will authenticate staff, and is MFA mandatory for all roles or selected roles?
5. Which managed PostgreSQL, queue/workflow, cache, object-storage and observability vendors are approved?
6. What are the approved availability target, RPO, RTO, backup retention and log retention periods?
7. Which one or two districts and circles form the pilot, and what device/browser inventory will they use?
8. Is Urdu required at pilot launch, and who owns translation, legal terminology and content approval?
9. Who is the named legal approver for the consolidated Act, rules, forms, schedule, account heads and notice wording?
10. Who owns L1, L2 and L3 support, payment reconciliation, security incidents and after-hours escalation?
11. What official ePay/e-Khidmat/treasury interfaces and sandbox credentials will be provided?
12. What migration scope, source registers, opening balances and reconciliation sign-offs are required for the pilot?
