# Scope

## Initial programme scope

- Staff authentication, role and jurisdiction administration
- Organization, district, office and circle master data
- Party/taxpayer registry and duplicate-resolution workflow
- Annual statement and assessment case management
- Hearing, decision, revision, withdrawal, discontinuance and appeal workflows
- Effective-dated tax schedule and legal configuration
- PFT-1/PFT-2/PFT-3 document and register capabilities after template approval
- Demand, adjustment, payment and reconciliation ledgers
- Service/delivery tracking
- Reports, dashboards, audit and exports
- Migration tooling and reconciliation
- ePay/e-Khidmat/treasury/identity/SMS/email adapters when contracts are approved
- Managed-cloud deployment, observability, backup and operations

## Not automatically in scope

- Other provincial taxes
- Unapproved public self-service
- Automated legal decisions
- Unapproved digital signature mechanisms
- Production use of provisional form wording or test schedules
- Predictive enforcement or automated recovery decisions
