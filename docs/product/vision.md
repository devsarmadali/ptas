# Product Vision

PTAS will provide one controlled, auditable and province-capable system for registration, assessment, demand, payment, adjustment, appeal, recovery monitoring and reporting of Punjab professional tax while preserving statutory decision authority.

## Outcomes

- A searchable party and taxpayer registry with duplicate review
- One annual assessment per taxpayer, with immutable revisions
- Effective-dated category/rate and form configuration
- Legally authorized decisions with hearing and appeal evidence
- PFT documents generated from approved snapshots
- Append-only demand and payment accounting
- Multi-channel payment reconciliation
- Role and jurisdiction-based work queues and dashboards
- Offline-resilient field capture where approved
- End-to-end auditability, accessibility and operational support
